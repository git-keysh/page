📌 Must-Know Formulas (rendered beautifully)

📐 Quadratics

  • ✔ Quadratic formula: $$x = \frac{-b \pm \sqrt{b^2-4ac}}{2a}$$
  • ✔ Discriminant: \(\Delta = b^2-4ac\)
    \(\Delta>0\): 2 real roots; \(\Delta=0\): repeated; \(\Delta<0\): no real roots
  • ✔ Sum of roots: \(\alpha+\beta = -\frac{b}{a}\)    Product: \(\alpha\beta = \frac{c}{a}\)
  • ✔ Completing square: \(a(x+h)^2 + k\)   where \(h = \frac{b}{2a}, k = c - \frac{b^2}{4a}\)

📈 Sequences & Series

  • Arithmetic: \(T_n = a+(n-1)d\)   \(S_n = \frac{n}{2}[2a+(n-1)d]\)
  • Geometric: \(T_n = ar^{n-1}\)   \(S_n = \frac{a(r^n-1)}{r-1}\)
  • Infinite sum (|r|<1): \(S_{\infty} = \frac{a}{1-r}\)

⚫ Circle Geometry

  • General form: \(x^2+y^2+2fx+2gy+c=0\)
    Centre: \((-f,-g)\)   Radius: \(r = \sqrt{f^2+g^2-c}\)
  • Standard form: \((x-h)^2+(y-k)^2 = r^2\)
  • Tangent at point: radius gradient \(m_r\) → tangent \(m_t = -\frac{1}{m_r}\)

📎 Vectors

  • Magnitude: \(|\mathbf{v}| = \sqrt{x^2+y^2}\)
  • Dot product: \(\mathbf{a}\cdot\mathbf{b} = |\mathbf{a}||\mathbf{b}|\cos\theta = a_1b_1 + a_2b_2\)
  • Unit vector: \(\hat{v} = \frac{\mathbf{v}}{|\mathbf{v}|}\)
  • Angle between: \(\cos\theta = \frac{\mathbf{a}\cdot\mathbf{b}}{|\mathbf{a}||\mathbf{b}|}\)

📐 Trigonometry

  • \(\sin^2\theta + \cos^2\theta = 1\)   \(\tan\theta = \frac{\sin\theta}{\cos\theta}\)
  • \(\sin(A\pm B) = \sin A\cos B \pm \cos A\sin B\)
  • \(\cos 2A = \cos^2A-\sin^2A = 1-2\sin^2A = 2\cos^2A-1\)
  • \(\sin 2A = 2\sin A\cos A\)   \(\tan 2A = \frac{2\tan A}{1-\tan^2 A}\)
  • Arc length: \(l = r\theta\)   Sector area: \(\frac{1}{2}r^2\theta\)

🧮 Calculus

  • \(\frac{d}{dx}x^n = nx^{n-1}\)   \(\frac{d}{dx}\sin(ax) = a\cos(ax)\)
  • Chain rule: \(\frac{dy}{dx} = \frac{dy}{du}\cdot\frac{du}{dx}\)
  • \(\int x^n dx = \frac{x^{n+1}}{n+1}+C\;(n\neq -1)\)
  • \(\int \sin(ax)\,dx = -\frac{1}{a}\cos(ax)+C\)
  • Area: \(\int_a^b f(x)dx\)   Volume (x-axis): \(\pi\int_a^b [f(x)]^2 dx\)

📊 Probability & Stats

  • \(P(A\cup B)=P(A)+P(B)-P(A\cap B)\)
  • Conditional: \(P(A|B)=\frac{P(A\cap B)}{P(B)}\)
  • Independent: \(P(A\cap B)=P(A)P(B)\)
  • Mean \(\bar{x}=\frac{\sum x}{n}\)   Variance \(\sigma^2 = \frac{\sum(x-\bar{x})^2}{n}\)
  • Log laws: \(\log(ab)=\log a+\log b\), \(\log a^n = n\log a\)
⭐ Linear reduction: \(y = ax^n \Rightarrow \log y = \log a + n\log x\)    \(y = ab^x \Rightarrow \log y = \log a + x\log b\)

📌 Past-paper style questions with full solutions

Q1 (Inverse function)
\(f(x)=\frac{2x+p}{x-1},\; x\neq 1\). (i) Find \(f^{-1}(x)\). (ii) Given \(f^{-1}(8)=5\), find \(p\).
✔ Step-by-step:
Let \(y = \frac{2x+p}{x-1} \Rightarrow y(x-1)=2x+p \Rightarrow yx - y = 2x + p\)
\(yx - 2x = p + y \Rightarrow x(y-2) = p+y \Rightarrow x = \frac{p+y}{y-2}\)
Therefore \(f^{-1}(x) = \frac{p+x}{x-2}\).
(ii) \(f^{-1}(8)=\frac{p+8}{8-2}=5 \Rightarrow \frac{p+8}{6}=5 \Rightarrow p+8=30 \Rightarrow p=22\).
Q2 (Sum & product of roots)
Quadratic \(2x^2+6x+7=0\) has roots \(\alpha,\beta\). Find \(\frac{1}{\alpha}+\frac{1}{\beta}\).
✔ Solution:
\(\alpha+\beta = -\frac{6}{2} = -3,\quad \alpha\beta = \frac{7}{2}\).
\(\frac{1}{\alpha}+\frac{1}{\beta} = \frac{\alpha+\beta}{\alpha\beta} = \frac{-3}{7/2} = -3 \times \frac{2}{7} = -\frac{6}{7}\).
Q3 (Geometric series – salary)
Year 3 = \$53,982.80, Year 5 = \$60,598.89 (geometric). Find first year and total over 5 years.
✔ Solution:
\(T_3 = ar^2 = 53982.80,\; T_5 = ar^4 = 60598.89\).
\(r^2 = \frac{60598.89}{53982.80} \approx 1.1225 \Rightarrow r \approx 1.0595\).
\(a = \frac{53982.80}{1.1225} \approx 48087.00\) (first year).
\(S_5 = a\frac{r^5-1}{r-1} \approx 48087 \times \frac{1.0595^5-1}{0.0595} \approx \$271,110\).
Q4 (Differentiation – equation of normal)
Point \(P(-2,0)\) lies on \(y=3x^3+2x^2-24x\). Find equation of normal at P.
✔ Solution:
\(\frac{dy}{dx}=9x^2+4x-24\). At \(x=-2\): \(9(4)+4(-2)-24 = 36-8-24=4\) (tangent slope).
Normal slope \(m_n = -\frac{1}{4}\). Equation: \(y-0 = -\frac{1}{4}(x+2)\) → \(y = -\frac{1}{4}x - \frac{1}{2}\).
Q5 (Circle & line intersection)
Circle \(x^2+y^2+4x-2y-20=0\) and line \(y=4-x\). Find intersection points.
✔ Solution:
Substitute \(y=4-x\): \(x^2+(4-x)^2+4x-2(4-x)-20=0\)
\(x^2+16-8x+x^2+4x-8+2x-20=0\) → \(2x^2-2x-12=0\) → \(x^2-x-6=0\)
\((x-3)(x+2)=0 \Rightarrow x=3 \Rightarrow y=1;\; x=-2 \Rightarrow y=6\).
Points: \((3,1)\) and \((-2,6)\).
Q6 (Probability without replacement)
Bag: 6 red, 4 green marbles. Two draws without replacement. Probability both same colour.
✔ Solution:
P(both red) = \(\frac{6}{10}\times\frac{5}{9}=\frac{30}{90}=\frac{1}{3}\).
P(both green) = \(\frac{4}{10}\times\frac{3}{9}=\frac{12}{90}=\frac{2}{15}\).
Total = \(\frac{1}{3}+\frac{2}{15}=\frac{5}{15}+\frac{2}{15}=\frac{7}{15}\).

🧠 Exam Question Patterns & Problem-Solving Steps

📌 Quadratic Inequalities

Example: \(\frac{2x+3}{x+1} \ge 0\).
Method: Critical points: numerator=0 → \(x=-1.5\); denominator=0 → \(x=-1\). Sign chart. Exclude \(x=-1\). Solution: \(x \le -1.5\) or \(x > -1\).

📌 Circle Tangent

Example: Tangent to \(x^2+y^2-4x+6y+8=0\) at \((3,-1)\).
Find centre \((2,-3)\), radius slope = \(\frac{-1+3}{3-2}=2\) → tangent slope \(-\frac12\). Equation: \(y+1 = -\frac12(x-3)\).

📌 Vectors Angle

Given \(\mathbf{p}=2\mathbf{i}+3\mathbf{j},\mathbf{q}=\mathbf{i}+5\mathbf{j}\).
Dot: \(2*1+3*5=17\), magnitudes \(\sqrt{13},\sqrt{26}\). \(\cos\theta = \frac{17}{\sqrt{13\cdot26}}\). Then \(\theta = \cos^{-1}(\frac{17}{\sqrt{338}})\).

📌 Kinematics (calculus)

\(s=t^3-2t^2+t-1\). \(v=\frac{ds}{dt}=3t^2-4t+1\). Set \(v=0\) to find rest: \((3t-1)(t-1)=0\) → \(t=\frac13,1\).

📌 Linear Law (log)

Given \(P = A x^{-k}\). Take logs: \(\log P = \log A - k\log x\). Plot \(\log P\) vs \(\log x\) → gradient = \(-k\), intercept = \(\log A\).

📌 Volume of revolution

Curve \(y=x^2+1\) from \(x=0\) to \(2\), about x-axis: \(V=\pi\int_0^2 (x^2+1)^2 dx = \pi\int_0^2 (x^4+2x^2+1)dx\).
