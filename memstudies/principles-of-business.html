Core Business Principles

1. Nature of Business

Barter, money functions, instruments of exchange (cheques, cards, e-transfers). Business forms: sole trader, partnership, co-op, companies, franchise. Economic systems: traditional, command, free market, mixed. Private vs public sector.

2. Internal Org Environment

Management functions: planning, organising, directing, controlling. Leadership styles (autocratic, democratic, laissez-faire). Conflict sources & resolution (mediation, arbitration). Trade unions, motivation, communication.

3. Establishing a Business

Entrepreneur functions, business plan elements (executive summary, operational, marketing, financial). Feasibility study, location factors, collateral. Steps: conceptualisation, research, funding, operation.

4. Legal Aspects

Contract essentials: offer, acceptance, consideration, intention, capacity. Simple vs specialty contract. Insurance principles: indemnity, insurable interest, utmost good faith, subrogation. Business documents: invoice, requisition, statement.

5. Production

Factors: land, labour, capital, enterprise. Productivity vs production. Cottage industries, small business roles. Levels: subsistence, domestic, surplus, export. Linkage industries (backward/forward).

6. Marketing

Marketing mix (4Ps): product, price, place, promotion. Market research, consumer behaviour, branding, packaging, sales promotion, customer service, intellectual property (trademark, copyright, patent).

7. Logistics & Supply Chain

Distribution channels: manufacturer → wholesaler → retailer → consumer. Multimodal/intermodal transport. Documents: bill of lading, airway bill. Technology: GPS, GIS, Portnet. Problems: spoilage, delays, theft.

8. Business Finance

Financial institutions: central bank, commercial banks, credit unions, insurance. Savings vs investment. Short-term (trade credit, overdraft) vs long-term (mortgage, debentures). Sources: personal savings, loans, venture capital. Basic records: P&L, balance sheet.

9. Role of Government

Government responsibilities: security, welfare, job protection, environment. Taxation (direct: income tax; indirect: VAT/GCT). Government assistance: grants, training, research. Social services: health, education, NIS.

10. Technology & Global Business

ICT in business (e-commerce, e-business, CAD). Standard of living vs quality of life. National income (GDP, GNP, per capita). Trade agreements: CARICOM, CSME, WTO. Economic problems: debt, unemployment, migration, dualism.
