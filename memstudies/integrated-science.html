📌 Complete Syllabus Recap (CXC 2025)

Cells & Life Processes

Diffusion, osmosis (water potential), active transport (ATP). Plant cell: cell wall, chloroplasts, large vacuole. Animal cell: centrioles, smaller vacuoles. Photosynthesis: 6CO₂ + 6H₂O → C₆H₁₂O₆ + 6O₂. Respiration: C₆H₁₂O₆ + 6O₂ → 6CO₂ + 6H₂O + 36-38 ATP.

Excretion & Homeostasis

Lungs (CO₂), skin (sweat: water, salts, urea), kidneys (urea, excess ions, water). ADH from pituitary increases water reabsorption. Dialysis mimics kidney function. Egestion = undigested waste (feces).

Energy & Simple Machines

Work (J) = force × distance. Power (W) = work/time. MA = Load/Effort. Efficiency = (MA/VR)×100%. Levers: class 1 (scissors), class 2 (wheelbarrow), class 3 (tweezers). Friction: both helpful (brakes) and harmful (wear).

Reproduction & Health

Asexual: cuttings, runners, bulbs, tissue culture. Sexual: pollination (self/cross), fertilisation, seed formation. Menstrual cycle: follicular phase, ovulation (day 14), luteal phase. Hormones: oestrogen, progesterone. Contraception: barrier, hormonal, IUD, sterilisation. STIs: HIV, gonorrhoea, syphilis. Immunity: vaccines, antibodies.

Earth & Environment

Soil profile: O, A, B, C horizons. Sand (large particles, low nutrients), clay (small, high water retention), loam (ideal). Soil pH affects nutrient availability. Erosion control: terracing, contour ploughing, windbreaks. Hurricanes form over warm ocean (>26°C). Volcanoes: lava, ash, pyroclastic flows. Tides caused by moon's gravity.

Electricity & Circuits

Ohm's law: V=IR. Power: P=IV, P=I²R. Series: same current, voltage divides. Parallel: same voltage, current divides. Fuse: thin wire melts on overcurrent. Earth wire prevents shocks. Conductors (copper, aluminium), insulators (rubber, plastic). kWh = energy used by 1kW device in 1 hour. LEDs > CFL > filament in efficiency.

Typical animal cell as seen under light microscope

🔬 Cell structures & functions

  • Nucleus: contains DNA, controls cellular activities.
  • Mitochondria: site of aerobic respiration, produces ATP.
  • Ribosomes: protein synthesis (attached to RER or free).
  • Cell membrane: selectively permeable, fluid mosaic model.
  • Cytoplasm: gel-like matrix, site of metabolic reactions.
  • Lysosomes: contain digestive enzymes (not shown but vital).
