Project Overview

What this project is

Nexus Command will work with A+ Academy to design, develop, and prepare native mobile applications for Android and iPhone. The apps will present the academy's content, contact points, and key information in a fast, reliable, and professional mobile experience.

The project covers initial development for both platforms, plus optional publishing and hosting arrangements that let you control distribution costs. You can publish on the official stores or host the installers directly from a website — whichever fits your budget and audience best.

Transparent pricing: No hidden fees. You pay for development, and you choose how you want to distribute the app. We will guide you through the options.
