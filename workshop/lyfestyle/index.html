Our Story

Where simple things are extraordinary

01

The Beginning

Simplicity Creations was first conceptualized and registered in 2015, starting out as a small home-based business. In the early days, the business focused on car lighting, including headlights, fog lights, and underglow kits. Over time, we slowly expanded into car audio products and installation, offering customers great products at affordable prices.

02

Our First Store

Simplicity Creations Couva opened in February 2023, officially launching our first storefront. This accomplishment marked the expansion of the Simplicity Creations brand into the central area. We were able to expand our line of products while still maintaining quality and affordability. Customers now enjoy a comfortable environment to shop and receive on-the-spot consultations from our in-house installer, Adrian Marajh.

03

Our Latest Chapter

Arima is the birthplace of Simplicity Creations, so it was only fitting that we finally opened a storefront to serve the East. Conveniently located in D'Abadie, this expansion marks another exciting milestone in our journey, allowing us to continue growing the brand while providing quality products, professional installation, and exceptional customer service to an even wider community.
